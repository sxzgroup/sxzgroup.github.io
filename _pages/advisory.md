Due to increasing interest from industry, startups, investment organizations, and research institutions, external collaborations are managed through a structured engagement process.

Depending on the scope and objectives, collaborations may take the form of academic exchanges, technical consultations, project support, or long-term advisory engagements. 

This helps ensure that sufficient time and attention can be dedicated to each collaboration.

---

## Areas of Expertise

We welcome collaboration and advisory inquiries in the following areas:

- **Quantum Computing**: Quantum algorithms, quantum AI, NISQ devices, quantum noise, quantum simulation, quantum error mitigation and quantum information.
- **Quantum Software**: Core infrastructure, design of quantum software, integration with ML frameworks. Optimization, feature request and deployment of TensorCircuit-NG.
- **Tensor Networks**: Quantum state representation, tensor network contraction, MPS/PEPS algorithms, quantum inspired applications.
- **HPC & GPU**: Vectorized parallel computing, GPU optimization, distributed training.
- **Scientific Computing Systems**: Large-scale simulation, automatic differentiation, JIT compiler integration, python and open source.
- **AI for Science**: Interplay between machine learning and quantum physics/engineering.

## Academic & Community Support (Free Channels)

To maintain active engagement with students, researchers, and the open-source community, the following options are available:

- **Community Support**: General questions or bug reports related to **TensorCircuit-NG** should be raised through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/tensorcircuit/tensorcircuit-ng/issues) and [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/tensorcircuit/tensorcircuit-ng/discussions) to benefit the whole community.
- **Office Hours**: Monthly open office hours are reserved for students and academic researchers for non-profit projects. Please email to request a booking slot.

## Recognition & Attribution

For collaborations that substantially benefit from technical guidance, software contributions, or intellectual input, appropriate acknowledgement, attribution, or advisory recognition may be requested where applicable.
